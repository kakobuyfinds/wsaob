Buying guides

Check UIDBuy product links before ordering

Open the original Weidian or Taobao listing and select the size and color yourself. For a 1688 listing, check the minimum order quantity and confirm whether the displayed price is per item or per pack. Save the seller item ID, selected variant and date so you can spot a stale spreadsheet link or a listing that has changed.

Compare the fit of UIDBuy fashion finds

For shoes, measure both feet and use the longer foot when checking the seller’s size chart in centimeters. Compare hoodie chest width, T-shirt length and jacket sleeves with pieces you already own. For pants, compare waist and rise. Check whether the seller measured garments flat, especially for jerseys and adjustable caps.

For accessories, check bag dimensions, scarf size and fabric, and necklace chain length. A photo alone rarely gives you a reliable sense of scale.

What to look for in UIDBuy QC photos

If warehouse photos are available, compare the exact variant with the original listing. For shoes, check both size labels and look at the heels side by side. For clothing, check the seams and ask for a tape-measure photo if fit is uncertain. For a bag or choker, ask for a clear view of the zipper or clasp before sending the parcel.

Compare parcel quotes for your destination

Use the final carton measurements to compare actual and volumetric weight under each carrier’s rules. If you plan to consolidate orders, request the dimensions for the combined parcel. Check service coverage for your postcode, then include the item total, domestic delivery, agent fees, international freight and any applicable import charges when comparing the delivered cost.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UIDBuy spreadsheet?

It is a list of product leads, typically organized by category. Reopen the original marketplace listing to confirm the seller, selected option and current availability.

Where do the category links go?

Each category opens its matching collection on CAFANS. This page is an independent guide and has no official affiliation with UIDBuy or the featured brands.

Can QC photos confirm authenticity?

QC photos help check visible details, condition and sizing. For authenticity, seek verifiable provenance or a qualified authentication service.

When can I get a useful UIDBuy shipping estimate?

Use the packed parcel weight and outer dimensions once available. Compare quotes for the same destination, parcel and service terms.
